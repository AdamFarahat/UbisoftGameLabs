## Cassini M. 
### A prototype for Ubisoft GAMELAB by Concordia's Team Cassini M.

United by the Cassini Bind, Mech units Tethys and Hyperion defend a broken Saturn from a power-hungry space force.
In this 90's Mecha anime inspired co-op game, you and your friends will cleave, parry, blast and mow them down to get the highest score before meeting your inevitable end.

#### Required for play:
- 2 standard controllers (Dualshock, Xbox)
- 2 pairs of headphones
- Requires 2 players.
